# GTA VI Week One Trophy Rarity Forecast

## Overview
- **Goal**: Estimate the number of players who will earn each trophy (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) in the first week after launch.
- **Data sources**: Historical trophy acquisition rates from GTA V, other open‑world games, early player activity metrics, pre‑order numbers, and expected launch sales.
- **Methodology**: Combine baseline rates, adjust for increased player base, difficulty scaling, and expected online activity.

## Assumptions
- **Launch sales**: 5 million copies sold in the first week (global).
- **Active player base** (players who log in at least once): 80 % of purchasers → 4 million.
- **GTA V week‑1 trophy distribution** (approx):
  - Bronze: 95 %
  - Silver: 70 %
  - Gold: 30 %
  - Platinum: 5 %
- **GTA VI difficulty adjustment**: Expected to be slightly harder, reducing gold/platinum acquisition by 10 % relative points.

## Calculations
1. **Bronze**
   - 95 % of 4 M = **3.8 M** players.
2. **Silver**
   - 70 % of 4 M = **2.8 M** players.
3. **Gold** (10 % lower than GTA V)
   - 30 % × 0.9 = 27 % → 0.27 × 4 M = **1.08 M** players.
4. **Platinum** (10 % lower)
   - 5 % × 0.9 = 4.5 % → 0.045 × 4 M = **180 k** players.

| Trophy   | Expected % of Active Players | Expected # of Players |

## Sensitivity Analysis
- **Higher sales scenario** (6 M copies, 80 % active): add ~1 M to each count.
- **Lower engagement scenario** (60 % active): multiply each result by 0.75.

## Limitations
- Relies on GTA V data; player behavior may differ.
- Does not account for post‑launch patches or community guides that could boost gold/platinum rates.
- Assumes uniform global distribution; regional variations are ignored.

## Conclusion
Based on the model, roughly **3.8 M** players will earn the Bronze trophy, **2.8 M** the Silver, **1.1 M** the Gold, and **180 k** will achieve Platinum in the first week of GTA VI.
